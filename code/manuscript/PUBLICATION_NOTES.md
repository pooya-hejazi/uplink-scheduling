# Publication and reproduction notes

The current article is **Bounding the rate loss of individual wireless uplink schedules**, by Pooya Hejazi and Masoud Sabaei, prepared for Nature Communications. The seven-note supplement contains sixteen tables, eight figures and 51 references. The point-by-point response accompanies the official-class paper in paper_submission/paper.

The November 14 reviewer extension adds absolute-rate comparisons, physical optimality brackets, a three-extra-iteration deterministic control, an O1 fixed-depth refinement sweep, and chronological RENEW residual calibration with exhaustive equal-power scheduling. New scalar outputs and scripts are in revision/reviewer_data and revision/reviewer_*.py. Original confirmatory inputs, results and releases remain unchanged.

Exact assignment of a rounded upper table is not an exact continuous-power optimum. Equal price-iteration counts are not equal execution time. Small-instance reference brackets have narrow medians but some wide tails. Chronological hardware coverage describes the observable later pilot in one capture per mode; it does not establish exchangeability or payload-channel coverage. Restricted measurement samples and reconstructed complex channels are excluded from distributable materials.

The standalone scalar phase-shape experiment is absent from the current article and supplement. Confidential editorial overlap disclosures remain in paper_submission/paper/editorial_only, outside the code/data repository. Both related manuscripts remain under review; no public preprint links have been invented.

P.H. conceived the study, formulated the mathematical bounds, developed the scheduling and verification software, analysed the simulation and measurement data, and wrote the manuscript. M.S. supervised the research, contributed to the problem formulation and analytical design, and reviewed and edited the manuscript. Funding is declared in Acknowledgements. The repository is pooya-hejazi/uplink-scheduling and is to remain private during review. This preparation does not submit anything to the journal or invite external reviewers.

Final prose and layout polish corrects the verification-table label, restores the full supplementary timing label, widens the signed-score columns and simplifies the Article wording. All numerical data files remain unchanged.
