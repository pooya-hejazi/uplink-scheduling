\paragraph{Noise scale of the stored pilot differences.}
We pooled the 1,614 HUB and 1,513 LO test frames, six users, 64 and 63
retained streams, respectively, and three tones. This gives 1,859,328 HUB
and 1,715,742 LO complex entries. For each user, frame and tone we aligned
the later vector using
\(\theta^*=\arg\sum_a h^{(0)*}_a h^{(1)}_a\).
We summarized both \(|h^{(1)}-h^{(0)}|^2\) and
\(|e^{-\mathrm{i}\theta^*}h^{(1)}-h^{(0)}|^2\), with equal weight per entry.
Their pooled means and medians, and
\(10\log_{10}(\operatorname{mean}|\Delta h|^2/
\operatorname{mean}|h^{(0)}|^2)\), appear in Supplementary Table~13.
The denominator is a pooled mean, not a mean of individual ratios.
Stream-by-tone summaries and frame-level means accompany the Source Data.
Projection quantiles pool 19,368 HUB and 18,156 LO individual terms
\(\big||d^Hh^{(1)}|-|d^Hh^{(0)}|\big|\), for unit-norm stored filters,
before the 12-term frame maximum. Reconstructed maxima agree with every
archived phase-invariant test score; both input digests match the original replay.

The raw mean powers, 18.398 and 121.622, exceed the independent unit-variance
pilot-noise prediction of two; phase alignment reduces them to 12.249 and
43.954, respectively. Mean early powers are 1,256.871 and 1,074.461 in
quiet-slot whitened units, so aligned mean relative errors of $-20.112$ and
$-13.882$ dB can coexist with large absolute projection residuals. These are
descriptive pilot-to-pilot ratios, not measurements of transmitter EVM.
Signal-dependent distortion, synchronization jitter, multipath leakage
between extraction windows, correlated errors, or a quiet-slot variance
that understates pilot-period noise could contribute. Stored channel pairs
do not separate these mechanisms or identify active-period noise variance.
Consequently, these hardware rates are conditional on the frozen quiet-slot
noise metric. If that metric understates active-period noise and distortion,
rates may be optimistic; gaps, pass fractions and floor ratios must then be
recomputed with the appropriate model. The present diagnostic does not
establish a correction factor or an unconditional upper bound on physical rates.
